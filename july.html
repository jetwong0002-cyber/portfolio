July.

Closing the internship through daily worker training, final system testing, documented handover, and reflection on how the working AI-assisted workflow could be used responsibly by others.

Week 01Daily training

Continuing worker training every day.

I maintained the training and induction routine through my final month, helping workers understand site requirements before beginning work.

  • Prepared the training space and checked attendance.
  • Explained site rules, high-risk activities, and safety expectations.
  • Answered worker questions and followed up on required information.
Week 02Final testing

Preparing the working system for handover.

The AI-assisted decision system entered its final testing stage, with attention on reliability, understandable outcomes, and the team’s day-to-day use.

  • Checked core data-entry and tracking workflows from end to end.
  • Reviewed dashboard outcomes, exception states, and final issues.
  • Prepared the working version for transfer to the team.
Week 03Documentation

Delivering the User Manual.

I documented the main workflows so the team could continue using and maintaining the system after my internship.

  • Explained how to enter, review, and update induction records.
  • Included guidance for the dashboard and common follow-up actions.
  • Submitted the completed User Manual to the team for handover.
Week 04July birthdays

Celebrating with the company and my colleagues.

The company held its July Babies celebration, and my colleagues also took time to celebrate my birthday with me.

  • Joined the company-wide birthday celebration for July employees.
  • Shared a personal birthday moment with colleagues.
  • Ended the internship feeling appreciated and connected to the team.
July takeaway: A decision-support project is not finished when the code works; it must also be understandable, reviewable, and usable by the next person. Testing, documentation, and handover helped me close the internship responsibly.
