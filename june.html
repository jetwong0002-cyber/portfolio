Month 03 · Longmotive internship

June.

A month of repeated daily processes: conducting inductions, checking the warehouse, coordinating workers, and noticing where consistent records and visible follow-up are important.

June cover photo images/june-induction-session.jpg
Week 01Daily induction

Conducting induction every working day.

Worker induction remained one of my main daily responsibilities throughout June.

  • Prepared the room and checked attendance before each session.
  • Helped workers understand essential site rules and safety expectations.
  • Recorded and followed up on worker information after induction.
Week 02Warehouse checks

Checking stock and keeping the warehouse orderly.

Alongside induction work, I continued checking the warehouse so materials remained organised and easier to locate.

  • Checked stock condition, quantities, and storage locations.
  • Kept frequently used items arranged for daily access.
  • Flagged shortages or follow-up needs for the team.
Week 03Worker management

Helping workers stay informed and organised.

I supported the day-to-day coordination of workers by answering questions, reinforcing instructions, and helping information move clearly between people.

  • Guided workers through induction and site requirements.
  • Helped resolve communication questions when they arose.
  • Kept worker information and follow-up actions organised.
Week 04Team connection

Spending time with my department outside the site.

Our department went out for a team-building dinner, giving us time to connect in a more relaxed setting.

  • Joined colleagues for a shared meal after work.
  • Built stronger working relationships with department members.
  • Returned to the daily routine with better team connection.
June takeaway: Repetition made process gaps easier to recognise. Daily induction, warehouse checks, and follow-up work showed the value of consistent fields, clear statuses, and an exception path when information is incomplete or uncertain.
