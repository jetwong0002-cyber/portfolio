Month 02 · Longmotive internship

May.

A hands-on month focused on how clear information supports safe decisions: leading a toolbox briefing, continuing operational duties, and completing practical safety training.

May cover photo images/may-toolbox-scissor-lift.jpg
Week 01Toolbox meeting

Leading a toolbox meeting on scissor-lift safety.

On 5 May, I helped lead the mass toolbox meeting and taught workers how to approach scissor-lift use safely.

  • Explained the key precautions before operating the equipment.
  • Supported practical instruction on safe scissor-lift use.
  • Helped keep the large workforce briefing organised and clear.
Week 02Daily operations

Applying safety awareness to daily site support.

I continued the regular operational responsibilities that keep the site organised while reinforcing the lessons from the toolbox session.

  • Maintained storekeeping records and stock organisation.
  • Supported ordering and material coordination.
  • Continued worker-induction and office coordination duties.
Week 03Fire safety

Learning to use a fire extinguisher.

I took part in practical fire-safety training and personally learned how to operate an extinguisher correctly.

  • Learned the correct preparation and operating sequence.
  • Practised directing the extinguisher safely during the exercise.
  • Built confidence in responding to a workplace fire emergency.
Week 04Work at height

Completing a hands-on work-at-height assessment.

On 26 May, I completed the practical Work at Height Simulator experience rather than only observing the demonstration.

  • Wore the required fall-protection equipment and followed the safety sequence.
  • Personally climbed onto the scaffold and descended safely.
  • Completed the practical requirement needed to qualify.
May takeaway: Decision support is not only a dashboard; it also depends on information that people can understand and act on. Teaching others strengthened my communication, while practical exercises showed why instructions must be clear, timely, and grounded in the field.
