Month 01 · Longmotive internship

April.

Learning the site’s operating rhythm: reliable daily support, clear communication, and a first chance to represent the team at workplace events.

April cover photo images/april.jpg
Week 01Settling in

Learning how daily site support connects.

I began by understanding how materials, orders, office tasks, and workers move through the site each day.

  • Supported storekeeping and checked the organisation of stock.
  • Helped maintain ordering records and follow-up coordination.
  • Assisted with office cleanliness and task coordination.
Week 02Daily rhythm

Supporting a consistent working environment.

My focus was on dependable daily operations and helping workers receive the information they need.

  • Continued storekeeping and material-ordering support.
  • Coordinated office tasks to keep shared spaces orderly.
  • Supported worker inductions, especially for Chinese workers.
Week 03Earth Day

Using language and coordination skills for an event.

On 21 April, I supported the Earth Day activity by preparing announcement wording and serving as the Chinese-language emcee.

  • Prepared the event announcement script.
  • Hosted in Chinese to support clear participation.
  • Continued core store, order, and induction responsibilities.
Week 04Safety & health

Helping a workplace event run smoothly.

On 28 April, I joined the World Day for Safety and Health at Work activity.

  • Distributed event T-shirts to participants.
  • Helped maintain an organised and orderly environment.
  • Continued daily support for office and worker coordination.
April takeaway: Strong operations rely on small tasks being completed consistently. I learned to support both the physical flow of materials and the human flow of communication.
