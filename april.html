Month 01 · Longmotive internship

April.

My first month combined learning how to support worker inductions, managing warehouse routines, coordinating fire-extinguisher servicing, and contributing to Earth Day and World Day for Safety and Health at Work.

April cover photo images/april-earth-day-team.jpg
Week 01Settling in

Learning the worker-induction process.

I started by observing how inductions are prepared and delivered so every worker receives the right site information before beginning work.

  • Learned the sequence and key information used during induction.
  • Observed how attendance and worker details are checked and recorded.
  • Built confidence in communicating instructions clearly to workers.
Week 02Site support

Managing the warehouse and servicing arrangements.

I supported the day-to-day organisation of the warehouse and coordinated with the appointed vendor to collect fire extinguishers for maintenance.

  • Checked and organised warehouse stock for easier daily access.
  • Gathered the fire extinguishers and prepared them for collection.
  • Arranged the vendor handover so servicing could proceed in an orderly way.
Week 03Earth Day

Taking the microphone as an event emcee.

On 21 April, I helped the Earth Day activity run smoothly by supporting the announcements and serving as one of the event emcees.

  • Prepared the event announcement script.
  • Used the megaphone to guide participants through the programme.
  • Joined the full-team Earth Day 2026 photo.
Week 04Safety & health

Supporting a site-wide safety and health event.

On 28 April, I joined the World Day for Safety and Health at Work activity.

  • Distributed event T-shirts to participants.
  • Helped maintain an organised and orderly environment.
  • Took part alongside the wider site team and workforce.
April takeaway: My first month showed me that site safety depends on both good systems and clear communication. I learned how induction, warehouse control, equipment servicing, and workforce engagement support one another.
